How the site fits

Long, narrow lot: ~153' of paved lot in front, the 195' building, then ~135' of gravel yard and the detention basin behind. The ground falls ~4' from front to back, so the slab is at grade at the entry and dock-height at the back. Neighbors: Case Lumber (east) and Graphic Design (west).

Original (as-is)

The property as it stands: original 72' × 121' metal building with the two-story office block up front, the 2006 Kirby crane bay behind it with a dock door and a depressed drive-in bay on the back wall, gravel back yard 4' below the slab.

Using the 3D model

  1. Left-drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
  2. Use the Views buttons (or keys 1–0) to jump to the street, rear, inside each suite, the truck well, or the office floors.
  3. Turn on Section cut (key C) and drag the slider to slice the roof off at any height — the office-floor views do this for you.
  4. Click anything — a wall, door, the crane, a parking surface — for its dimensions and the source note.
  5. Switch versions with the pills at the top right; the budget page follows the same version.
